What this prototype covers
  • Engine: Krumhansl key detection, per-bar chord scoring (long and accented notes weigh more), a beam search over progressions that prefers strong moves (V → i, IV → V, root falling a fifth), and smooth voice leading.
  • Key lock: click an alternative or pick tonic and scale yourself. Auto-detect returns to the detector.
  • Scales: major, minor, harmonic minor, Dorian, Phrygian, Mixolydian, Lydian. With borrowed chords on, minor keys also get the major V, and major keys get iv, ♭VI, ♭VII and secondary dominants.
  • Harmonic rhythm and length: 2 chords per bar, 1, or 1 per 2 bars; 4, 8 or 16 bars; 4/4, 3/4 and 6/8.
  • Lock and regenerate: lock the chords you like, then Regenerate changes only the rest. Picking an alternative locks it.
  • Undo, redo and saved progressions (saved in this browser only).
  • Clashes: melody notes that fight the chord turn red, and the line under the cards says why each chord was picked.
  • Style changes the rhythm: Pop pulses, Trap holds long chords, Lo-Fi swings, Jazz comps with a walking bass.
  • MIDI: import a .mid as the melody; export a .zip with full, chords, bass and melody .mid files.
